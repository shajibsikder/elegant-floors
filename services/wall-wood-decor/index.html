Frequently Asked Questions About Wood Wall Decor

Is WPC wood paneling resistant to Kuwait's heat and humidity?
Yes. WPC (Wood-Plastic Composite) is specifically formulated to withstand high humidity and extreme summer temperatures without warping, rotting, or color degradation.
Can panels be installed over existing painted walls without demolition?
Absolutely. Our technicians affix panels directly onto your existing wall surface using heavy-duty German adhesive systems and hidden fasteners without damaging structural masonry.
Where are the best spots to install wood wall paneling?
Popular residential locations include entryway feature walls, dining alcoves, living room accent walls, hallway galleries, and bedroom headboard backings.
