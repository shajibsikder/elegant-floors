أنواع خامات الباركيه المتوفرة في الكويت

خشب طبيعي صلب (Solid Hardwood)

أرضيات خشب طبيعي 100% مثل البلوط والزان والجوز، تمنح الفلل والمنازل فخامة لا تضاهى مع إمكانية الصنفرة والتجديد لسنوات طويلة.

خشب هندسي شبه طبيعي (Engineered Wood)

طبقات خشبية متقاطعة عالية الثبات، تمنح مظهر الخشب الطبيعي مع مقاومة استثنائية للتمدد والانكماش تحت أجهزة التكييف المستمرة.

Frequently Asked Questions — Parquet Flooring in Kuwait

Can parquet flooring withstand Kuwait's high heat and humidity?
Yes. We install engineered and treated laminate parquet featuring moisture-resistant cores and heavy-duty underlayment specifically suited for Kuwait's climate and continuous indoor AC usage.
How long does parquet installation take?
A standard bedroom or living room takes only 2 to 4 hours. An entire apartment is typically completed within a single working day without any disruption.
Can parquet be installed directly over existing ceramic tiles?
Yes. We clean and prepare the tile subfloor, lay high-density acoustic foam underlayment, and install the floating parquet directly on top without removing existing tiles.
How should I maintain and clean my parquet floor?
Clean regularly with a soft broom or vacuum, followed by a well-wrung damp microfiber mop. Avoid excessive standing water and harsh chemical cleaners to preserve its factory finish.
