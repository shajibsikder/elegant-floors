Modern TV Wall Decor Materials & Features in Kuwait

At Elegant Floors Kuwait, we build bespoke television wall backgrounds integrating high-grade materials with modern European design aesthetics:

Engineered C-Board Panels

High-strength structural boards engineered for fire-resistance, durability, and secure anchoring of modern large screens from 55 to 98 inches.

Wood & Marble Alternative Combos

Combining fluted wood slats (WPC) with glossy UV marble sheets creates a luxurious architectural focal point at a fraction of natural marble weight.

Integrated Concealed LED Lighting

Warm indirect LED backlighting reduces eye strain during movie watching while transforming your living room into an upscale theater ambiance.

Key Advantages of Custom TV Wall Paneling

100% Concealed Cable Management

All cables, HDMI cords, consoles, and power outlets are completely hidden behind the panels for a sleek, clutter-free minimalist finish.

Heat & Scratch Resistant

Materials withstand heat emitted from continuous screen and receiver usage without discoloration or warping under Kuwait indoor climate.

Clean Fast Setup

Once panels are fabricated, on-site installation at your villa or apartment takes only one day with zero mess or wall demolition.

Frequently Asked Questions — TV Wall Decor in Kuwait

Can heavy large TVs be safely mounted onto the C-Board?
Yes. We install heavy-duty reinforced internal backing studs and steel mounting brackets rated to support all screen weights from 55 up to 98 inches securely.
How are wires and electrical cables hidden?
All wiring, receiver cords, and power cables are channeled through dedicated concealed internal conduits behind the decorative panels.
How long does TV wall decor installation take?
Once dimensions are taken, on-site installation is completed within a single working day without disruptive wall chipping or dust.
