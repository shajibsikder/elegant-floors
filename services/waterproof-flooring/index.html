Frequently Asked Questions About Waterproof SPC Flooring

Can SPC flooring be installed over old ceramic tiles?
Yes. SPC's rigid core bridges minor subfloor imperfections and clicks directly over level existing tiles without removal, saving time and demolition expenses.
Is SPC flooring suitable for kitchens and laundry areas?
Yes. SPC is the leading choice for kitchens and laundry rooms because it is 100% impervious to moisture, easy to disinfect, and stain-resistant.
What is the difference between laminate and SPC flooring?
Laminate has a wood fiber core that swells when exposed to water, whereas SPC has a stone-polymer core that never swells or buckles even if completely submerged.
How long does SPC flooring last in Kuwait's climate?
High-grade SPC flooring lasts 15 to 25 years with standard residential care, maintaining its pristine texture and finish.
