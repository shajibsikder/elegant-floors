Our 3D Wall Panel Installation Process in Kuwait

1

Wall Prep & Symmetry Centerline

Surface cleaning, primer sealing, and chalking precise centerlines to ensure flawless balanced symmetry across the entire wall.

2

High-Tack Adhesive Bonding

Application of heavy-duty polymer adhesive to secure each 3D tile firmly in place with micro-level alignment checks.

3

Seam Caulking & Texture Blending

Filling tile joints with flexible acrylic filler and precision sanding to eliminate visual breaks between panels.

4

Priming & Fine Finish Painting

Spraying or rolling high-opacity primer and customer-chosen finish paint, followed by room cleanup and inspection.

Frequently Asked Questions About 3D Wall Panels in Kuwait

Can 3D wall panels be painted in custom colors to match room decor?

Yes. Our 3D decorative panels can be painted in any color using high-quality acrylic, latex, satin, or metallic paint finishes, providing total design flexibility.

Are 3D wall panels durable against daily household contact?

Yes. When professionally installed with high-tack adhesive and sealed seams, composite plant-fiber and polymer panels are sturdy, impact-resistant, and ideal for living rooms, master bedrooms, and office feature walls.

How are the seams between 3D tiles made invisible?

We apply flexible, paintable joint compounds along every tile interface, followed by micro-sanding prior to painting to create an unbroken sculptured wall surface without visible seams.

How long does a 3D feature wall installation take in Kuwait?

A typical feature accent wall of 15 to 25 square meters is installed, caulked, primed, and painted within 1 to 2 working days.
