Frequently Asked Questions About TV Wall Decor in Kuwait

Can heavy TV wall brackets and large screens be mounted safely on C-board panels?

Yes. Our technicians anchor heavy-duty brackets directly into the structural masonry wall or reinforced studs behind the decorative panels, easily holding screens up to 85+ inches securely.

How are unsightly HDMI and power cables hidden?

We build dedicated concealed raceways and cable conduits behind the panels before mounting, ensuring zero visible wires between your TV, soundbar, gaming consoles, and power outlets.

Are the decorative materials heat-resistant and suitable for Kuwait homes?

Yes. The composite C-boards, UV marble sheets, and WPC slats are moisture-proof, fire-retardant, and resistant to temperature shifts caused by continuous AC operation in Kuwait.

How long does a complete TV wall unit installation take in Kuwait?

Most residential living room and diwaniya TV background installations are finished within 1 to 2 working days, including slat alignment, LED ambient backlighting, and final clean-up.
