Frequently Asked Questions About Flooring & Wall Decor in Kuwait

What flooring materials perform best in Kuwait's climate?

For areas exposed to moisture or temperature fluctuations, 100% waterproof SPC (Stone Plastic Composite) flooring is ideal. For living areas and bedrooms, AC4/AC5 rated European laminate parquet installed with vapor barriers provides excellent durability and thermal stability.

Can you install parquet flooring over existing ceramic tiles without demolition?

Yes. Parquet and SPC flooring can be installed directly over existing clean, level ceramic or porcelain tiles using acoustic underlayment, avoiding dust and demolition costs.

What is C-Board in TV wall background decoration?

C-Board is a sturdy, moisture-resistant composite substrate used as the foundation for modern TV accent walls. It allows safe wall mounting of heavy TVs up to 85+ inches and provides hidden channels to conceal cables.

Do you provide on-site measurements and quotes across Kuwait?

Yes. Elegant Floors provides free on-site inspections, accurate measurements, and material sample viewings across all governorates including Kuwait City, Hawalli, Salmiya, Farwaniya, Ahmadi, and Jahra.
