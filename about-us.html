Who is Elegant Floors Kuwait?

Elegant Floors Kuwait (woodflooringhouse.com) is a premier specialized contractor dedicated to supplying and installing high-grade wood parquet flooring, 100% waterproof SPC rigid core flooring, modern TV backgrounds, and decorative wall paneling throughout the State of Kuwait.

Backed by seasoned master craftsmen and certified technicians, we execute residential villas, apartments, commercial offices, and traditional Kuwaiti diwaniyas to international standards. We select every flooring board and wall panel specifically to withstand Kuwait's intense climate challenges — from extreme summer heat and coastal humidity to constant temperature transitions caused by continuous central air conditioning.

Certified master installers with 10+ years Kuwait hands-on experience
Direct factory-grade European AC4/AC5 laminate & 100% waterproof SPC
