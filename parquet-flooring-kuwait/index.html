Frequently Asked Questions About Parquet in Kuwait

Can parquet flooring be installed over existing ceramic tiles?
Yes. Parquet planks install directly over clean ceramic or porcelain tiles using specialized underlayment, eliminating messy tile demolition.
How does central air conditioning affect parquet in Kuwait?
Large temperature differences between outdoor heat and indoor AC can cause improper floors to buckle. We install calibrated expansion gaps and moisture barriers so boards expand and contract naturally without damage.
What is the difference between AC4 and AC5 wear ratings?
AC4 is ideal for heavy residential spaces like living rooms and bedrooms. AC5 offers extra abrasion resistance suited for commercial offices, busy reception areas, and diwaniyas.
How long does installation take?
Most standard apartments or residential living rooms (up to 60-80 sqm) are completed, trimmed, and tidied within a single working day.
